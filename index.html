Frequently asked questions

How long does the initial review take?

The form takes about one minute. A specialist will then review the information and contact you to discuss potentially relevant options.

Who may qualify for a physician mortgage?

Eligibility varies by program and lender. Physicians, dentists, residents, fellows, and certain other medical professionals may qualify.

How does up to 100% financing work?

Qualified borrowers may be able to finance the full purchase price through an eligible physician-loan program. Availability depends on program guidelines, location, loan size, and underwriting.

Can student loan debt be considered differently?

Potentially. Some physician-loan programs use specialized guidelines when calculating or evaluating student loan obligations.
